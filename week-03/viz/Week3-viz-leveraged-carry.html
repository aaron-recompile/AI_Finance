The formula: ROE = shigh + (shigh − blow) × (leverage − 1). At 1× you just earn the high supply rate; each extra turn adds one more spread. That is why degens loop.

Leverage has a ceiling: the position collateralizes the loan, so LTV = (ℓ−1)/ℓ must stay under the collateral factor → ℓ ≤ 1/(1−CF) (CF 0.85 → ~6×). At the ceiling HF≈1: one small adverse move and you are liquidated.

Leverage cuts both ways. Drag the Low borrow rate above the High supply rate (a rate shock): the spread goes negative, debt outgrows the position, HF slides to 1, and your equity is wiped faster the more leverage you used. Fragility = leverage (§28).

The dream → flash loan. This carry needs equity locked as a cushion and can be liquidated. What if you could borrow the whole position with zero equity, capture the spread, and repay in the same transaction? No cushion, no liquidation (atomic) — but only for instant, self-closing arbs. That is the flash loan, next.
