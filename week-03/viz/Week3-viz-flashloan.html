Zero capital. You never owned the borrowed tUSDC — you got it and gave it back inside one transaction. No collateral, because there is nothing to seize: if you don't repay by the last line of the tx, the chain rewinds the whole thing as if it never ran.

All-or-nothing (atomicity). Borrow, buy, sell, repay are one indivisible action. Profit ≥ 0 → it commits, you keep the leftover. Profit < 0 → the repay line fails → everything undoes. Your only real cost is gas. No holding, no HF, no keeper can catch you — there is no "position" to catch.

Why more borrow ≠ more profit. Buying MAX on the cheap DEX pushes its price up; selling on the dear DEX pushes its price down. Your own trades close the very gap you're eating (price impact / §08). Past the optimum, the gap is gone and fees dominate → profit turns negative → revert. The arb is self-limiting; the pool's depth caps your take.

Why this can't be done by hand. Manually you'd need three separate transactions — and after step 1 you'd be holding an uncollateralized loan the chain would never have given you. Atomicity only exists inside one tx, so a flash loan must be a contract. This is the door from "click by hand" to "write a program."
