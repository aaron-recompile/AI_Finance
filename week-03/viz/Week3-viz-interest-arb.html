The one idea (iEarn, 2020): on-chain yields are public readable state, and moving your deposit is a permissionless function. So "put my stablecoin wherever it earns most" can be written as an unattended contract — no bank, no counterparty approval.

Why the gap closes (convergence): when you supply into the high-APR venue, its utilization drops (more cash, same borrowers) → its supply APR falls. Pile in enough and the three venues equalize. That is a market becoming efficient — just slowly, over time, unlike a swap arb that snaps shut in one trade.

Re-open a gap: drag a venue's borrow-demand slider up (a big borrower arrives) → its utilization and APR jump → chase again. This is what a rebalancing vault would do for you automatically — the next step.
