Flash loan · the crown

Borrow → arb → repay, in one transaction

  • Start with $0 of your own
  • Borrow a pile, arb the gap, repay — all in one atomic call
  • No collateral: if you don't repay, the chain rewinds everything
open flashloan-viz.html → Execute at 4,000
Flash loan · the rule

All-or-nothing (atomicity)

Profit ≥ 0 → it commits, you keep it
Profit < 0 → the whole tx reverts, you lose only gas
Borrow too much → your own trades flatten the gap (price impact) → revert. Self-limiting.
