The big idea

Whoever fires first closes the door

The opportunity is public. The first bot to act takes it — and the gap vanishes for everyone else.
That race is MEV. Good MEV (arbitrage, liquidation) fixes the market and earns a fee. Bad MEV (sandwiches) preys on you.
