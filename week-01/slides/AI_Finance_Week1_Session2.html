The key picture · keep this

Moving a token is not moving ETH

ETH → goes person-to-person on the chain's ledger. Token → you poke the contract, it edits two rows. No ETH moves.

AI Finance · Week 1
Zoom out · what did you just do?

You issued money — a privilege that used to belong to states

1976 · Hayek — Denationalisation of Money: money needn't be a state monopoly; let issuers compete.
1994 · Szabo — coins "smart contract": code that enforces its own terms (the vending machine).
2009 · Satoshi — a trustless public ledger — but scripts kept deliberately minimal.
2015 · Vitalik + ERC-20 — a world computer + a token standard → issuing becomes one command.
2017 · USDT/USDC + the ICO rush — private dollars on-chain; billions raised on a whitepaper (most → 0).
Today · You — one line of code: 888,888 AARON.
The barrier to issuing an asset fell from "be a sovereign" to ~zero.
AI Finance · Week 1
The unresolved tension

Zero-friction issuance = liberation and a breeding ground

Liberation

  • Permissionless capital formation, global, 24/7.
  • Private money competing — Hayek's dream.
  • A dollar that never sleeps (USDC).

Breeding ground

  • 2017 ICOs: mostly went to zero; rampant scams.
  • "Wildcat" coins backed by nothing — like your AARON.
  • Same risks as 1800s private banknotes.
The old questions never left: who backs it? who controls it? You saw Circle's power inside the USDC proxy — it can mint · blacklist · upgrade. Bitcoin = no issuer (sound money); Ethereum = anyone issues. The irony: crypto set out to escape state money, yet its most-used asset is a tokenized dollar.
