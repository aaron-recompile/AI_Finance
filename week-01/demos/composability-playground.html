Off = real contract: the Token has no owner hook; a swap always completes as written. Turn it on to reveal an “Owner: intercept & keep funds” button — and see why “code is law” matters.

Mental model: You → AMM → Token is one atomic transaction — a call stack that nests and unwinds. Each machine runs only its fixed, public rule. That contracts can freely call other contracts is composability: the superpower you’ll exploit (flash loans, Week 3) and the attack surface you’ll defend (MEV / security, Weeks 4 & 6).
