Part A · Hyperliquid

A perp is spot + a spring

No expiry. The funding rate pulls the perp back to spot: when perp > spot, longs pay shorts.
So holding the short side earns funding — as long as you hedge away the price risk.
Same carry, on-chain

Long spot · short perp · collect funding

long POBTC spot + short BTC perp
→ direction cancels (delta ≈ 0)
→ funding + basis − fees = P&L
Last week the basis closed itself at expiry. A perp never expires — the funding rate is what pulls it to spot, and holding the short earns it.

The fence · written as hard limits

When it must close or stop

  • basis back below 0.3% → the edge is gone → close
  • net loss past −$10 → circuit-break → force close
  • held 7 days → close · spot bid empty 3× → alert
  • a STOP file → close and halt
The signing key is an API wallet: it can trade, but cannot withdraw or transfer. The fence is in code, not in hope.
Part B · Polymarket

A market on what happens next

"Will this exec leave by Q3?" — the price is the crowd's probability. Trade it like any other price.
New venue, same instinct: when the price and the real odds disagree, there's an edge.
